On September 6, 2026, [my first pull request](https://github.com/0xlau/biliplus/pull/228) was merged.

It may not be a particularly popular project, and the change itself may have been small, but that does not make the moment any less meaningful to me. This was the first time I had submitted a pull request and seen it accepted by a maintainer.

I opened my first issue sometime around the middle of 2025. Back then, I barely knew how to describe a problem properly, let alone follow an issue template. I simply wrote down what had gone wrong and submitted it.

A developer replied surprisingly quickly. We worked through the reproduction steps together, confirmed that it was indeed a bug, and eventually got it fixed. Even though we lived in different time zones, the collaboration felt smooth and genuinely enjoyable.

After going through the process of submitting this pull request, I finally understood what makes the open-source community so special: people who may never have met can come together around a shared problem, exchange ideas, and leave something a little better than they found it.

I am very happy that this was my first experience contributing to open source.
