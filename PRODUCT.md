# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Readers of zetazero.me, visiting from desktop or mobile to read personal essays and technical notes.

## Product Purpose

Serve a personal blog with a quiet, text-first reading experience. Success means the author's public posts are easy to browse, open, and read on the custom domain.

## Brand Personality

Personal, thoughtful, understated. The visual reference is bendrucker.me: dark editorial typography, compact navigation, and restrained orange links.

## Anti-references

Do not use a generic SaaS landing page, dashboard cards, bright gradients, or unrelated activity/photo content from the source repository.

## Design Principles

- Let the writing lead the interface.
- Keep navigation small and predictable.
- Preserve the reference site's typographic rhythm and dark reading surface.
- Use only the user's own public blog content.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Respect reduced-motion preferences, maintain readable contrast, and keep the content usable on narrow screens and with keyboard navigation.
